# Changelog

## 1.1.1 (25 November 2026)

- The status bar's clock and icons are readable again in light mode on Android 15 and newer: the app now draws edge to edge, with system bar icons following the system's light or dark theme.

## 1.1.0 (24 November 2026)

- Status tiles in each room: devices, sensor groups and checks the owner chose, coloured by level (temperatures blue to red), explained on a tap.
- Actions that don't run because their conditions don't hold say why.
- Pause alerts (⋮) for people the owner allows.
- ⋮ → Try the demo also once paired; the demo shows status tiles.

## 1.0.0

First release, prepared for Google Play.

- Pairing by QR code or typed code, at home or away from home, with the hub's key checked before anything is sent.
- Rooms as tabs to swipe between, actions as tiles coloured by what they switch; a tap runs an action.
- Long press on a tile: what the action touches. Long press on a room tab: the room's devices.
- At home and away: the home address on Wi-Fi, the outside address elsewhere, announced by the hub or typed.
- Demo with a sample home, for trying the app without a hub.
- About page with the licence, privacy policy and support links.
- Stored data is versioned and upgraded step by step; the token is never in backups.
